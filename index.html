About AviTech Solutions

Technology support built around your needs.

AviTech Solutions is a technology services company focused on helping individuals, businesses and organisations adopt reliable, practical and affordable technology. We provide professional IT support, computer and networking services, CCTV and security systems, access control, biometric and time & attendance solutions, digital business services and custom software solutions. Our services cover the full technology lifecycle, from consultation, installation and configuration to troubleshooting, maintenance, upgrades and ongoing technical support. Whether you need a reliable office network, a security system, a professional website or day-to-day IT assistance, we provide solutions tailored to your operational requirements and budget. We believe technology should solve real problems rather than create unnecessary complexity, which is why our approach is centred on practical solutions, dependable support, clear communication and long-term maintainability. We also provide flexible freelance technical support for individuals, businesses and organisations requiring assistance with specific projects or technical tasks. Based in Uganda, AviTech Solutions understands the local business and operating environment and is committed to making professional technology services accessible to growing businesses, institutions and individuals.
